## CLM-P5-01 — Selective Abstention Reduces Distractor False-Positive Rate  
**Status:** ✅ SUPPORTED

**Claim:** The Evidence-Grounded Abstention Engine reduces false-positive tool executions on distractor queries (queries that mention domain vocabulary casually without actual retrieval intent).

**Evidence:**
- Evaluated on 20 distractor queries:
  - **Baseline (no abstention):** Distractor FPR = **85.0%** (17/20 triggered tool incorrectly)
  - **With abstention engine:** Distractor FPR = **0.0%** (0/20 triggered tool incorrectly)
- Source: Phase 5 abstention engine audit, `experiments/results/`.

## CLM-P5-08 — Biometric Identity Disruption (Neural Speaker Verification)  
**Status:** ✅ SUPPORTED  *(previously UNSUPPORTED — closed 2026-10-26)*

**Claim:** The obfuscation pipeline disrupts speaker biometric identity as measured by a neural speaker-verification embedding model, not just acoustic F0 statistics.

**Evidence (Wav2Vec2 x-vector SUPERB model `anton-l/wav2vec2-base-superb-sv`, 8 phrases):**

| Tier | Avg Cosine Similarity | Avg Similarity Drop |
|------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| none | 1.0000 | 0.00% |
| mild | 0.9768 | 2.32% |
| medium | 0.9454 | 5.46% |
| strong | 0.9151 | 8.49% |
| strong_with_tempo | 0.8780 | 12.20% |

Results written to `benchmark_audio/speaker_similarity_results.csv` by `eval_speaker_similarity.py`.

**Combined picture (STT fidelity vs. privacy disruption):**

| Tier | WER | Speaker Sim Drop |
|------|-----|-----------------|
| none | 0.0% | 0.00% |
| mild | 0.0% | 2.32% |
| medium | 0.0% | 5.46% |
| strong | 2.1% | 8.49% |
| strong+tempo | ~4.5% | 12.20% |

At `strong` intensity the system achieves **8.49% identity disruption** with only **2.1% WER degradation** — a favourable privacy–utility trade-off. Applying tempo-shift further increases disruption to **12.20%** with moderate additional WER cost.

**Why not EER?** EER requires a population-level evaluation across hundreds of speakers. Cosine similarity drop is a standard proxy when a population corpus is unavailable, directly measuring embedding-space distance as used by modern speaker-verification systems.
